# Privacy Policy — Ando

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Ando ("the app", https://ando.fit) is a running and activity-tracking
application operated by x2flip (sole proprietorship, United States).

## What we collect

- **Activity data you choose to sync**: workouts (runs, walks, hikes),
  including GPS tracks, heart rate, distance, pace, elevation, and
  timestamps, imported from sources you connect (such as Garmin or
  Android Health Connect) or files you upload.
- **Account data**: your email address and display name, used for sign-in
  and identifying your content.

## How we use it

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features to you: displaying
your activity history, maps, statistics, and any social features you
choose to participate in.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or
  marketing.
- We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to the app's features.

## Storage and control

Activity data is stored on the operator's own infrastructure. You can
delete individual activities or your entire account at any time from
within the app; deletion removes your content from all app surfaces.

## Third-party sources

When you connect a data source (e.g., Garmin), the app only reads the
data you authorize, and your use of that source remains governed by its
own privacy policy. Disconnecting a source stops all further reads.
